Pocket Piggy and Chorsee: how they compare

By the maker of Pocket Piggy · Published September 30, 2026 · Updated October 3, 2026

Chorsee is a subscription chore tracker that runs on Android and can require photo proof. Pocket Piggy costs $9.99 once, puts the family chore board on your Apple TV, and is built around a real allowance system: Spend, Save and Give jars and a weekly Pay Day. Here is how to pick.

Download Pocket Piggy

Free for one kid · $9.99 once for the whole family · no subscription

The short answer

  • Pick Chorsee if anyone in the family uses Android, you want a photo before a chore counts, or you'd rather reward with points than money.
  • Pick Pocket Piggy if your family is all Apple, you're done paying for subscriptions, and you want kids to learn what to do with money, not just earn it.

What you get with Pocket Piggy

  • The chore board on your TV. The whole family's week on the living room screen: one row per person, each day's chores and who's done, balances and a Pay Day countdown. The Apple TV app is free. Chorsee's listing doesn't include Apple TV.1 See the Apple TV board.
  • An allowance system, not just a balance. Earnings split automatically into Spend, Save and Give jars, with savings goals, giving to a named recipient and optional interest you pay on savings. Chorsee tracks one balance in allowance or points, with manual adjustments and an optional automatic weekly or monthly allowance.1 See the allowance features.
  • Pay Day. Money moves on a day you pick, with a countdown and a celebration. Approve a chore late and that Pay Day fixes itself.
  • Read-aloud for little kids. Kid Mode reads the screen and each chore out loud, so kids who can't read yet can use it on their own.
  • Parents in the rotation. Grown-ups can take turns on chores with the kids.

What you get with Chorsee

  • Android. Chorsee's website links to a Google Play app.2 Pocket Piggy is Apple only.
  • Required photo proof. Chorsee can require a photo for specific chores.1 In Pocket Piggy a photo is optional; a parent approves every chore either way.
  • Points or no rewards. Chorsee lets you use points instead of money.1 Pocket Piggy tracks money.
  • Kids on their own devices. Chorsee has a child mode for kids' phones and tablets, and widgets that can check off chores.1 In Pocket Piggy, kids check off chores in Kid Mode on a family iPhone or iPad, behind a parent's PIN, and widgets are view-only.

What they share

Both track chores that can belong to one person, rotate, or be up for grabs, and neither links a bank account or card.1 You pay your kids however you already do. Pocket Piggy rotates weekly or monthly, not daily.
