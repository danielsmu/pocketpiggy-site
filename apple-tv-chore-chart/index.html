The family chore board, on your Apple TV

Put everyone's chores on the biggest screen in the house. Pocket Piggy on Apple TV shows the family board in the living room, in step with your iPhone and iPad.

Why put chores on the TV?

The best chore chart is the one everyone actually sees. A chart on a phone is easy to forget, and paper on the fridge gets buried under school notes. The TV is where the family already gathers. Put the board there and the week's chores are part of the room, not something you have to go looking for.

It also changes the conversation. Instead of "did you do your chores?" you can point at the screen. Kids can see their own progress, see what's left, and see that a sibling has chores too. That last part heads off a lot of "it's not fair".

What the family board shows

The Apple TV app shows the family board: each person's chores for the week, side by side, so the whole family can see the plan at once. Chores that take turns show up for whoever's turn it is, and chores set to everyone show up for each person. Grown-ups who are assigned chores show up on the board too, which kids tend to find very satisfying.

Always in step with your phones

Pocket Piggy syncs through iCloud. When a kid checks off a chore on the iPad and you approve it on your iPhone, the board on the TV keeps up. There's nothing to update by hand and no separate list to maintain. You set up chores on iPhone or iPad, where typing is easy, and the TV takes care of showing them.

A few ways families use it

  • The morning check. Put the board up while everyone gets ready, so kids can see what needs doing before school.
  • The after-dinner review. Look at the board together, talk about what got done, and approve chores on your phone while everyone is in the room.
  • Weekend planning. Saturday morning is a good time to put the board up and agree who is doing what before the day disappears.
  • The countdown to Pay Day. If your family uses the allowance features, the whole week builds toward Pay Day, and the living room is a fun place to watch it arrive.

How to set it up

  1. Download Pocket Piggy on your iPhone or iPad and add your family and chores.
  2. On your Apple TV, open the App Store and download Pocket Piggy.
  3. With the Apple TV signed in to the same Apple Account, your family's board syncs through iCloud.

The Apple TV app is included free, even with one kid. You don't need the Family Unlock to use it.

Still like paper too?

Some families like a copy on the fridge as well as the screen. Pocket Piggy can print a monthly chore chart, which is handy for the kids' rooms, for a grandparent's house, or as a backup for the days the TV stays off.

More than a TV app

The Apple TV board is one part of Pocket Piggy. On iPhone and iPad, parents approve chores, set up rotations and manage allowance, while kids use Kid Mode, locked behind a PIN, with read-aloud for kids who aren't reading yet. Home screen widgets put today's chores on your phone. Read more about the chore chart.
