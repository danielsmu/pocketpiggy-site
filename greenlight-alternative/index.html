Pocket Piggy and Greenlight: how they compare

Pocket Piggy is a chore chart and allowance tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Here is an honest look at how it differs from Greenlight, with every Greenlight detail linked to its official source.

Two different kinds of app

Pocket Piggy and Greenlight both help kids learn about money, but they do it in different ways. Greenlight is a financial technology company, not a bank; banking services are provided through Community Federal Savings Bank, Member FDIC. (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: fees)

Pocket Piggy is a chore chart and allowance tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. It doesn't hold money, issue cards or connect to a bank. It keeps a shared record of chores and what kids have earned, and you pay your kids however you already do. Because they work so differently, the right choice depends on what your family is looking for.

Greenlight at a glance

These details come from Greenlight's own website and help pages. Prices and features change, so check the linked source before you decide.

  • Plans start at $5.99/month for the whole family, including up to five kids. (as of September 2026; source: greenlight.com/plans)
  • Higher plans: Greenlight Max $10.98/month, Greenlight Infinity $15.98/month, Greenlight Family Shield $19.98/month, plus applicable taxes. (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: fees)
  • A non-customized debit card is included with all plans; a Custom Card is $9.99 plus applicable taxes. (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: fees)
  • Greenlight is a financial technology company, not a bank; banking services are provided through Community Federal Savings Bank, Member FDIC. (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: fees)
  • Higher plans include extras such as cell phone protection and purchase protection (Infinity). (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: Infinity plan)

Where Greenlight may be a better fit

  • A real debit card. A non-customized debit card is included with all plans; a Custom Card is $9.99 plus applicable taxes. (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: fees)
  • Banking services. Greenlight is a financial technology company, not a bank; banking services are provided through Community Federal Savings Bank, Member FDIC. (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: fees)
  • Protection plans. Higher plans include extras such as cell phone protection and purchase protection (Infinity). (as of September 2026; source: Greenlight help: Infinity plan)
  • Whole-family plans. Plans start at $5.99/month for the whole family, including up to five kids. (as of September 2026; source: greenlight.com/plans)

Where Pocket Piggy may be a better fit

  • No subscription. Pocket Piggy is free for one kid, and the Family Unlock is $9.99 once.
  • No bank account or card to link. Pocket Piggy is a ledger. It tracks what kids earn and you decide how they get paid.
  • A family board on Apple TV. Everyone's chores on the living room screen.
  • Chore rotation. Chores can take turns between kids, grown-ups included, or go to everyone.
  • A printable monthly chore chart for the fridge.
  • iPhone, iPad and Apple TV kept in step through iCloud family sharing.

Choosing for your family

If your goal is to give a kid real spending money on a card, Pocket Piggy isn't built for that. If your goal is a chore chart the whole family can see, with an allowance that keeps the numbers straight and nothing to link, Pocket Piggy may suit you.
