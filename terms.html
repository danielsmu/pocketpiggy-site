What Pocket Piggy is

Pocket Piggy is a record-keeping app for family chores and allowance. It is a ledger: it tracks what a household has agreed to pay its children. It is not a bank, a payment service, or a financial institution. No real money moves through the app, no account is opened, and no card is issued. Balances shown in the app are your family's own record of what is owed, and paying it is up to you.

The family unlock
