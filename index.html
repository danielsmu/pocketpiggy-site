Questions

Do my kids need their own phones?

No. Most families run Pocket Piggy on the parents' phones and hand it over when a kid wants to check something off. If a kid does have their own iPad or iPhone, you can set it up as a kid's device: it opens straight into Kid Mode and only a code you choose gets back out.

Does everything sync between parents?

Yes, through your own iCloud account. Turn on Family Sync, send the invite link, and every grown-up's device shows the same chores and the same balances. There's no Pocket Piggy account to create and no server of ours holding your family's data.

Is there real money involved?

No. Pocket Piggy is a ledger, not a bank. It tracks what you owe your kids so everyone agrees on the number. Nothing is connected to a bank account, no card is issued, and no money moves through the app. Payday happens however it already happens at your house.

What if we forget to check something off?

Approve it late and the app fixes the maths for you. If a chore belonged to a week that already paid out, Pocket Piggy works out what that week is now worth and pays the difference automatically.

Do you have to pay kids money for chores?

Not at all. Set a chore's amount to zero and it's simply something that needs doing. That suits grown-ups' chores, or for families who'd rather not tie money to every task. You can also pay one flat amount for the week instead of per chore.

What does the free version leave out?

Two things: a second kid, and sharing the family with another grown-up on their own Apple Account. Everything else is free with one kid: every chore type, Pay Day, goals, Kid Mode, read-aloud, widgets, Watch and Apple TV, synced across all the devices on your own Apple Account. When you add a second kid or invite another grown-up, you'll be asked to unlock the family, once, forever. No subscription.
