How to use a printable chore chart

  • Start with a few chores. Three to five a day is plenty. You can add more once the routine sticks.
  • Put it where everyone sees it. The fridge, a bedroom door, or next to the family calendar.
  • Check it at the same time each day. After dinner or before bed works for most families.
  • Use the Pay Day row. Add up the week together, whether you pay in money, stickers or a special treat.
